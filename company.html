Privacy notice

Who we are

Wanaka IT Solutions Kft. (the details above) is the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at info@wanakait.com.

What data we process

  • When you email us: your name, your email address and anything you include in your message.
  • When you visit this website: our hosting provider processes technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or any kind of tracking.

Why we process it, and on what legal basis

We use your message only to answer your enquiry and, if you wish, to prepare an offer or a contract. The main legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). If you write to us in your own name and ask for steps before a contract, the legal basis is also the steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

The technical data that our hosting provider processes when you visit this website is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in delivering this website and keeping it secure (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Do you have to give us your data?

No. You are not required to give us any personal data. Without your contact details, however, we cannot answer your enquiry.

Automated decisions

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages for as long as needed to answer them, and for at most 2 years from the day we receive them, if no business relationship follows. If a contract follows, we keep the related correspondence for 5 years after the contract ends (the general limitation period under Hungarian law), and documents that support our accounting records for 8 years.

Who else processes it

  • Website hosting: GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages). GitHub processes technical visitor data as described in its own privacy statement.
  • Email: Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft 365).

These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers rely on the safeguards required by the GDPR. Both providers state that they take part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Where that does not cover a transfer, standard contractual clauses apply. You can ask us for information about these safeguards.

We do not sell your data and we do not pass it to anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, its correction or deletion, the restriction of its processing, or a copy of it in a portable format, and you can object to its processing. Write to info@wanakait.com.

Complaints

You can lodge a complaint with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH), 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11, naih.hu, or turn to a court. You can also complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.
