Tailor

We develop and customise Business Central so that it is convenient for your people and works smoothly with the other systems in your company, from web shops and CRM to logistics partners and retail systems. Every extension is built to make the daily work of the user easier. Our work includes integrations with platforms such as ConnectWise and LogiSense.

  • CRM
  • Integrations

Improve

We are building a new way to upgrade and migrate Business Central systems, with AI as a second pair of eyes. It checks each step and writes the documentation, split by Business Central role, so every user gets only the information that matters for their work. Automated tests run through your processes before and after the move, so they carry over reliably.
